Hobbiton

Hobbiton is the Lord of the Rings film set in Matamata on the North Island, about 2 hours from Auckland. The standard tour is NZ$130 and takes 2.5 hours. Seeing it adds £75 per person in flights and costs 3 South Island nights. A slot for the dates is not confirmed.

Check before booking

  • Homebound connection in Brisbane is 1h40, on one ticket.
  • The internal flights are separate tickets, so a delay on one is not protected.
  • Hotel and car figures are estimates. The internal fares were not tested for 2 seats.
  • Jetstar fares exclude overhead bin access. Qatar's bag allowance was not checked.
  • Entry rules depend on passport.
